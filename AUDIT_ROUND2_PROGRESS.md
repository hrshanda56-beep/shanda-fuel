## Decisions made

- “Still to bank” and “cash at stations” mean the unallocated portion of a collection. Any amount allocated to a deposit is in transit, even before bank confirmation.
- “Settlements to receive” is the unmatched approved POS/EcoCash evidence amount, so Quick Look, the dashboard and Financial position use one figure.
- Unpaid expenses match the Expenses list’s posted manual-expense filter; recurring recognition and submitted approval items remain separate buckets.
- Movement inclusion for a dip follows the authoritative business date so a later approval does not move a sale out of the business day being measured.
