SUPERVISOR's current template supports submission but lacks reconciliation.approve. The product's accountable approving Supervisor is a business responsibility; resolve the actual authorized approver arrangement without broad privilege grants. Do not redefine requirements to match a role label.

## Staff access verification

- [ ] Real staff list approved; accounts invited through Settings Staff with actual station/entity scope.
- [ ] Each staff member accepts their own invitation and signs in with their own credentials.
- [ ] Configured provider and supported MFA enrollment/recovery confirmed; no codes/seeds shared with trainer.
- [ ] Attendant sees only allowed station/shift and permitted boundary capture.
- [ ] Supervisor can operate assigned day/shift and submit where permitted; cannot infer approval from role title.
- [ ] Finance sees authorized evidence/reports; Owner/Admin configuration access is scoped and justified.
- [ ] Independent approver has required permission, station scope and fresh supported verification.
- [ ] Wrong tenant/station and ungranted writes are denied in authorized synthetic testing; no unauthorized probe against real customer data.
- [ ] Disabled/expired access and sign-out procedure verified through the approved access process.
- [ ] Staff competency and escalation contacts recorded; no temporary broad grants remain.
