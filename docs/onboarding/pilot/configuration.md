## Required evidence bundle

Keep references in the restricted onboarding record: legal/station identity; Owner authorization; asset register and calibration/capacity evidence; product/tank/nozzle/meter map; shift schedule; active price evidence; physical opening stock measurement and signed valuation source; opening meter record; bank/provider ownership and cutoff statements; staff role/scope approval; actual command outcomes/state; discrepancy resolution; training record; final acceptance.

Do not attach production credentials, MFA material, invitation tokens, unmasked bank details or unnecessary staff personal data to repository documents. A screenshot is useful only when its build and scope are known and sensitive details are removed. Do not copy demo screenshots as proof of station configuration.

## Exceptions and interrupted operations

A failed response may hide a successful write. Inspect persisted state before retrying; retain the same unchanged operation/idempotency attempt where the supported UI offers it. A stale-version conflict requires reload and review. Never repeat financial postings with new identifiers to force success. Missing permissions require access-owner investigation, not borrowing an account. Approved/locked mistakes require a named correction or compensating workflow; no direct database edits.
