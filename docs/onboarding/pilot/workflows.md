## Readiness limitations that must remain visible

The summary now requires an active meter with a currently effective installation for every active nozzle. It still does not certify opening boundaries, sealed segments, stock quantity, authoritative valuation, timezone validity, physical tank/nozzle routing or the full independent staff/approver matrix. One station-scoped staff grant is not proof that every required role is staffed. Payment readiness currently requires both bank and provider accounts; this is an existing summary rule, not a newly imposed business requirement for every station.

The detailed setup blockers and summary both require an effective active price (start reached and end not reached). The detailed list still limits price rows to 200. Physical nozzle-to-tank routing is not represented by the current schema; verify it independently and escalate multi-tank ambiguity. Do not call an ACTIVE label alone sufficient.
