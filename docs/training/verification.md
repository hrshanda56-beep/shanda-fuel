## Onboarding blocks and unavailable capabilities

Before first live shift, the owner/station must provide actual organisation/entity/station, timezone, staff and scope, independent approver, shift templates, pumps/nozzles/meter installations, active prices, opening boundaries/stock evidence, payment/bank/provider setup, expense authorization/evidence process and escalation contacts. Use the onboarding workbook; do not invent production values or accounting/tax policy.

Not established as complete staff workflows: general offline draft synchronization; cross-device meter-close recovery; full attendant self-service handover; complete approved/locked correction UI; every customer lifecycle action; general ledger/tax/FX; full expense approval/allocation; automatic bank/provider integration; complete final profitability. Backend commands or a navigation item do not prove those capabilities are available end-to-end.

UI guidance gaps: B approval lacks C's integrated verification; B meter-close does not collect missing readings; selection of reconciliation attendants is distinct from nozzle responsibility; required customer/approved-fuel/custody attribution may need workflows beyond generic payment rows; interrupted-request error wording can overstate zero-write certainty. The SOPs require escalation rather than bypasses for these gaps.

## Maintenance and handoff gate

The integration owner owns the release-to-training mapping; the station's designated trainer owns copies, contacts and competency records. Before distribution:

1. Record actual deployed SHA and authentication provider through the approved release evidence process; this task does not authorize contact.
2. Confirm migration/permission state and independent approver arrangement with release/access owners.
3. Exercise role-specific flows on an authorized training build using synthetic actors, including denied actions, return/resubmit, MFA cancellation/failure, interrupted writes and incomplete reports.
4. Check phone/tablet/desktop accessibility and real button wording; capture screenshots only with a known verified build and sanitized training data.
5. Confirm local custody/safety/escalation procedures and all real onboarding facts; print-preview the quick starts.
6. Record trainer/owner acceptance and remaining gaps. Only then mark training ready for live assigned duties.

For updates, change the release register and affected guides/SOPs together. Validate local Markdown file links, referenced route files, command names and formatting, then obtain normal documentation review. Do not rewrite historical evidence or call unrun checks PASS.
