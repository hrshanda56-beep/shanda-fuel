# Training session and competency record

## Trainer preparation

Confirm exact released SHA and whether B or C instructions apply using [verification](verification.md). Confirm provider, role grants and real station setup with the integration owner. Obtain escalation contacts and approved local custody/safety procedures. Use an authorized synthetic training environment for exercises; this pack does not authorize environment access or real transactions.

Explain the difference between recording evidence, submission, independent approval, shift/day close, banking and settlement. Demonstrate unavailable/error states as well as success. No participant should use another person's account.

## Suggested 90-minute agenda

| Minutes | Activity                                                              | Evidence of learning                                                    |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–10    | Own-account sign-in, MFA, sign-out, station/entity selection          | Learner identifies correct context without exposing secrets.            |
| 10–20   | Role limits, day/shift states, real onboarding dependencies           | Learner identifies who records, submits, approves and escalates.        |
| 20–40   | Supervisor/attendant opening, meter verification and closing          | Learner selects correct nozzle and verifies saved evidence.             |
| 40–55   | Payment channels, expenses, cash count and differences                | Learner keeps channels separate and preserves real amounts.             |
| 55–70   | Save, submit, independent review, return/correct/resubmit             | Learner distinguishes unsaved edits from persisted state.               |
| 70–80   | Secure approval and reports; separate bank/provider follow-up         | Authorized learner verifies approval; others explain permission limits. |
| 80–90   | Network interruption, wrong reading, discrepancy and competency check | Learner stops safely and gives a useful escalation note.                |

Use role-specific groups for writes. A learner without the relevant grant observes or explains the step; do not elevate access for training convenience. Approval exercises need two legitimate synthetic actors. C-only recovery and MFA exercises require that exact candidate or an approved successor with the same verified behavior.
