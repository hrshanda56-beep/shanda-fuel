## Autonomous decisions

- Cash-bucket classification uses the authoritative current cash-location type. `STATION_TILL` and `STATION_SAFE` are physically at the station; `COLLECTOR` is management-held. Deposit-date reporting remains based on `bank_deposits.deposited_at`, while collection-date buckets remain based on collection business date.
- The in-transit financial-position row is shown as its own existing statement row, because this makes the asset sum explicit and avoids hiding a material amount in helper text.
- Negative stock is flagged in the existing Needs attention list instead of blocking late-dated operational entries; the seed supplies opening stock before the first sale.
- Staff/management fuel benefit is deducted from operating profit after ordinary and fixed expenses as a cost of the benefit, but it is not treated as cash.
- The UI-lock manifest was re-baselined for the explicitly requested wording/value corrections only; no layout, style, navigation, component structure or flow was changed.

## Needs a design decision

R3-B03, R3-B07, R3-B08 and R3-B10 remain explicitly listed above. They are not release-gate failures for the recorded v1 contract, but must not be silently presented as complete owner functionality.
